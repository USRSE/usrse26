## Things to Do or See
If you have time before or after the conference, we encourage you to get out and explore the city and Bay Area!

- [Japanese Friendship Garden](https://www.sanjose.org/listings/japanese-friendship-garden) - A quiet escape with koi ponds and walking paths inside Kelly Park.
- [Winchester Mystery House](https://winchestermysteryhouse.com/) - A famously bizarre Victorian-style structure with doors that open to wall and stairways that lead to the ceiling.
- [Sierra Vista Open Space Preserve](https://www.openspaceauthority.org/preserve/sierra-vista) - If you're looking to get out of the city, this hilltop offers hiking with panoramic views of Silicon Valley.
- [LEGOLAND Discovery Center Bay Area](https://www.legolanddiscoverycenter.com/bayarea/) - A place you can build more than software!

## Places to Eat
- [Poor House Bistro](https://poorhousebistro.com/) - Located close to the river, this eatery offers New Orleans inspired dishes.
- [Casa Guzmania](https://www.casaguzmania.com/) - This casual, colorful restaurant is highly rated and serves sweet and savory empanadas.
- [Fox Tale Fermentation Project](https://www.foxtalefermentationproject.com/) - Highly unique and beloved by locals, Fox Tale offers creative fermented foods, craft beer, and kombucha.
- [Angkor Chef](https://angkorchef.com/) - A short walk from the hotels, this Cambodian restaurant servers a variety of curries, stir fries, and noodle dishes.

More suggestions can be found at [sanjose.org](https://www.sanjose.org/things-to-do)
